Privacy Policy — Page Approvals for Confluence

App On · Last updated: 9 September 2026 This policy explains what the Confluence app Page Approvals for Confluence ("the app") does with data. It is deliberately short, because the app does very little with data.

The short version

The app stores nothing outside your own Confluence site. Everything it records — who approved what, when, at which page version — is written into Confluence itself, as content properties on your pages and a settings property on your space. If you uninstall the app, that data stays in your Confluence and the app takes nothing with it. There is no server of ours holding a copy. The app sends nothing to any third party. It makes no network calls outside Atlassian's own infrastructure, and it uses no artificial-intelligence service.

Where it runs

The app runs on Atlassian Forge, Atlassian's own hosting platform, inside Atlassian's infrastructure. It is not hosted by us and we operate no servers. Data residency follows your Atlassian site's own settings.

Logs

The app writes short diagnostic messages (for example, "the notification comment could not be posted") to the Forge logging service operated by Atlassian. These messages do not contain page content, approval comments, or user names. Atlassian retains them for a limited period and the app author can read them only for the purpose of fixing faults.

Comments the app writes

When someone requests approval, the app can post a comment on the page that mentions the approvers, so that Confluence notifies them in the usual way. This comment is ordinary Confluence content, visible to everyone who can see the page. A space administrator can turn this behaviour off in **Space settings → Integrations → Page approvals**.

Your rights, and how to exercise them

Because the app stores nothing of its own, requests to access, correct, export or delete data are handled by deleting or editing the relevant content in your Confluence site, which you control. Uninstalling the app removes the app's access immediately.

Sub-processors

None. The app uses no third-party service.
